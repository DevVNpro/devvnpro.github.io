# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2025

We operate various mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our Apps.  
By using our Apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information directly on our servers.  
However, our Apps use third-party services (such as Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, Google Sign-In, and Firebase) that may collect data as described below.

### 1.1 Automatically Collected Information
Third-party services may collect:  
- Device information (device model, OS version, hardware identifiers)  
- IP address  
- Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)  
- App usage data (how you interact with the app)  
- Crash logs and performance data (if using Firebase Crashlytics)  
- Analytics data (if using Firebase Analytics)  

### 1.2 Google Sign-In (Authentication)
If you sign in with Google, we may receive the following information from Google:  
- Google Account ID  
- Display name  
- Profile picture  
- Email address (only if you grant permission)  

We use this information only to provide features such as login, progress sync, and social features.

### 1.3 Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)
If you use leaderboards, the following data may be collected and displayed:  
- Player ID provided by Google  
- Display name  
- Profile icon  
- Scores, ranks, and achievements  

This data may be visible to other players.

## 2. How We Use the Information

We use collected information (via third-party services) for:  
- Providing advertisements  
- Improving app performance  
- Crash reporting and fixing technical issues  
- Analytics and usage measurement  
- Google login functionality  
- Displaying leaderboards and achievements  

We do not sell or share your personal information with marketers.

## 4. Data Retention and User Control

Leaderboard and Google Play Games data are handled by Google and follow Google’s retention policies.  
You may request removal of leaderboard data by contacting us (see Section 8).  
You may manage or delete your Google account data through your Google Account settings.  
We do not store personal data on our own servers unless explicitly required for gameplay features (and if so, we will specify it in app-specific policies).
